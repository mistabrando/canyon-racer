# TrackMania Canyon reference brief

## What makes it fun

- Short, repeatable time attacks turn every corner into a line-optimization puzzle.
- Failure is cheap: crash, restart immediately, and try the same segment again.
- The fixed car/physics make friend and ghost comparisons feel fair; improvement is attributable to execution.
- Drifting is planned before a corner, not triggered as a last-second rescue. Entry line, timing, slip, and exit alignment create mastery.
- Milliseconds matter, so PB ghosts, splits, and readable feedback provide the next concrete target.
- Stunts and elevation punctuate precision driving with spectacle and risk.

## Visual language to reference, not copy

- Monumental sandstone cliffs and plateaus create large silhouettes and a strong horizon.
- Smooth engineered roads, bridges, guardrails, loops, ramps, and arches contrast with rough natural terrain.
- Warm orange/red rock is separated from a cooler blue sky and pale concrete/metal track structures.
- Multiple terrain depths—roadside, midground formations, distant mesas—create scale and parallax.
- Landmarks and visible future track segments help players learn the route at speed.
- Barriers remain low and readable; scenery frames the racing line rather than becoming a corridor of boxes.
- Sparse vegetation and track furniture are accents, not the primary environment.

## Gameplay-loop targets for Canyon Racer

1. Start immediately or restart in one input.
2. Read the upcoming corner from road geometry, signs, terrain, and ghost position.
3. Choose a line and drift entry.
4. Commit, countersteer at the right moment, and feel the exit slingshot.
5. Crash or lose time in a consistent, understandable way.
6. See checkpoint/PB delta and immediately retry.
7. Share the same deterministic course plus a valid ghost.

## Non-goals

- Do not copy logos, branded track furniture, textures, or proprietary geometry.
- Do not add expensive post-processing, dynamic shadows, or dense unique meshes.
- Do not use visual noise to simulate speed when physics/camera/track rhythm are wrong.
