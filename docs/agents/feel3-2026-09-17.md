## Wall-press bypass — why contact keeps full press

With the ramp applied in contact, the weaker press let post-impact slides
persist into a wall-to-wall rattle: pin residual 3.5 -> 8.7..15.6, stuck latch
1050 -> 0, escape fwd 6.3 -> 1.0..4.0, R5 recovery never. Grind pace is
scrape-drag-dominated (press-insensitive — 13e/R5-grind bands held under every
variant), so full press in contact restores all verified wall behavior while
only free-road launch slows. Harness 13d/E6/R5-grind/11h/gripBound all green.
