## What was added (this pass)

board carries a code-native near-black chevron (`arrowMesh`, one instanced draw

  double-chevron span (1.5 + 0.5 = 2.0u) still fits the 2.4u board.
  Zero budget impact: same 2 tris per symbol, same one instanced draw call.
  Placement (`s`, `side`, advance/repeater) untouched, so road/rail exclusion
  and inside-apex clearance are unchanged; lateral arrow extent from board
  center grows 1.08u -> 1.25u, still far outside the rail at `halfW + 2.0`.
- `tests/visuals-budget.ts` — new section `8e` pins the legibility geometry

## How legibility was judged (headless)

- Contrast: near-black `0x101010` symbol on amber `0xf2a53a` / red `0xd83a2a` /
  deep-red `0xd11a0f` boards — maximum luminance separation, no texture.
- Direction: `8a` proves the arrow points into the turn against the track's own
  curvature (`dT/ds`), independent of placement side; `8d` fake-kit mount proves
  every arrow instance's `scale.x` equals its encoded `arrowPointSign`.
- Three-way separability: sweeper (amber, single) vs drift arc (red, single)
  vs tightening (deep red, double chevron) — shape AND color, per `8b`.
- Distance: new `8e` fill-ratio pins (symbol >= 50% of board width) as the
  headless proxy for chase-cam readability; symbol size up ~30% this pass.
